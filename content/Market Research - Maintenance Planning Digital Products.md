# Maintenance Planning Digital Products — Full Market Research & Product Ideation

**Date:** 25 June 2026
**Context:** Research conducted for building a digital product store selling PDF/Notion/Excel templates for maintenance professionals, leveraging real-world mining maintenance planning experience.

---

## Executive Summary

**The industrial/mining maintenance digital template niche is a blue ocean.** Of 12+ search queries for heavy equipment, mining shift reports, and industrial maintenance templates across Gumroad, Etsy, and Creative Market — nearly all returned **zero relevant products**. Combined with data from the Notion Marketplace (6,782 templates in Operations, but only 24 in fleet management), the gap is clear: industrial-grade maintenance templates are dramatically underserved compared to vehicle/home maintenance.

**Key competitive advantage:** Real-world experience as a maintenance planner at Evolution Mining. Every other template seller is a generalist designer — credibility is the product.

## Product Line Strategy — 9 Products

### Tier 1: Industrial/Mining Niche (Your Expertise = No Competition)

**Product 1: Mobile Equipment Maintenance Log — PDF/Excel**
*Target: Fleet supervisors, mobile mechanics, mine maintenance planners*
- Sections: Equipment ID & specs, daily pre-start checklist, PM schedule tracker, repair history log, parts used, labour hours, downtime tracking, next-service countdown, fuel/fluid top-up log, operator sign-off
- Format: Excel (interactive) + PDF fillable (printable)
- Your edge: Real mining equipment categories — haul trucks, excavators, dozers, graders, LHDs, drills
- Price: **$19–39 AUD**

**Product 2: End-of-Shift Maintenance Report Template**
*Target: Shift supervisors, maintenance planners, mine managers*
- Sections: Shift summary, work orders completed (planned vs unplanned), WIP/incomplete jobs, breakdown root cause, equipment availability %, safe production units, safety incidents/near-misses, parts shortages, handover notes, priority flagging
- Format: Excel (daily log + weekly summary sheet) + PDF printable
- Your edge: These are YOUR daily forms — you know exactly what data matters and what doesn't
- Price: **$15–29 AUD**

**Product 3: 13-Week Rolling Maintenance Schedule**
*Target: Maintenance planners, reliability engineers, asset managers*
- Sections: Asset register import, PM task library (by equipment type and frequency), rolling 13-week Gantt calendar view, resource allocation grid (fitters/electricians by trade), parts pre-order checklist, backlog management, weekly KPI dashboard
- Format: Excel or Google Sheets (interactive, formula-driven)
- Price: **$27–47 AUD**

**Product 4: Heavy Equipment Pre-Start Inspection Checklist Pack (10 Machines)**
*Target: Operators, mine sites, mobile plant supervisors*
- Checklists for: Haul truck, excavator, dozer, grader, LHD/loader, water truck, light vehicle, service truck, crane, drill rig
- Each checklist: Machine-specific (not generic), one-page, check-box format, critical items flagged, sign-off line, defect reporting section
- Format: PDF fillable (also printable for laminated use)
- Price: **$12–19 AUD for pack of 10**

### Tier 2: Broader Maintenance & Reliability

**Product 5: Preventive Maintenance Schedule Template (Industry-Agnostic)**
*Target: Facility managers, small business owners, fleet operators*
- Sections: Asset master list, PM task database, frequency matrix (daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly/annual), completion log, overdue dashboard, cost tracking per asset, estimate vs actual hours
- Format: Excel with conditional formatting + PDF reference guide
- Price: **$19–34 AUD**

**Product 6: Work Order Management System**
*Target: Self-managing workshops, small-to-medium maintenance teams*
- Sections: Work order creation form (auto-numbered), triage/priority matrix, labour allocation, parts/materials listing, cost estimation, completion sign-off, work order register, overdue tracking, weekly report
- Format: Excel or Google Sheets
- Price: **$17–29 AUD**

**Product 7: Maintenance KPIs & Performance Dashboard**
*Target: Maintenance managers, reliability teams, plant managers*
- KPIs: OEE calculator, MTBF / MTTR tracker, schedule compliance %, corrective vs planned ratio, cost per unit, backlog trend (open WO count), backlog value
- Includes: Pre-built charts, conditional formatting, month-on-month comparison, 12-month rolling view
- Format: Excel (dashboard tab + data entry tab)
- Price: **$22–39 AUD**

### Tier 3: Premium / Information Products

**Product 8: "Build a Maintenance Plan from Scratch" Workbook**
*Target: New maintenance planners, students, career switchers*
- Contents: 5 guided exercises, glossary of 50+ maintenance & reliability terms, template library (mini versions of core products), worked examples from mining & manufacturing
- Format: PDF workbook (40+ pages)
- Price: **$27–47 AUD**

**Product 9: Ultimate Maintenance Planner Bundle — Notion**
*Target: Serious maintenance professionals, plant managers, companies*
- Includes: PM Schedule DB + Work Orders DB + KPI Dashboard + Equipment Register DB + Spare Parts Inventory DB + End-of-Shift Report — all as one interconnected Notion workspace
- Databases: 6+ linked databases with relations, rollups, and formula fields
- Price: **$97–197 AUD**

### Bonus: Corporate / Site License Products (B2B)

- Multi-site maintenance planner bundle — 5+ site licenses with shared asset register
- Mine-site onboarding template pack — SOPs + pre-starts + shift reports + PM schedules for a new mine opening
- Audit-readiness compliance pack — ISO 9001 / 55001-aligned maintenance documentation templates
- Price: **$497–997 per site** (one-time, with 1-year updates)
